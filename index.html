Places to visit — Bari

⛪ Basilica di San Nicola

Must-See · Free

One of the most important churches in Italy — built in 1087 to house the relics of St Nicholas (the original Santa Claus!). Magnificent Romanesque architecture, remarkable crypt. Pilgrims come from all over the world. Go early morning.

🌐 Site📍 Map
📍 Piazza San Nicola, Bari Vecchia · ⏰ 7am–8pm · 💶 Free

🏘️ Bari Vecchia (Old Town)

Free · Get lost!

A labyrinth of narrow medieval alleyways, archways, laundry hanging overhead and women making pasta at their doorways. One of the most authentic old towns in southern Italy. Best explored without a map.

📍 Map

🍝 Strada delle Orecchiette

Free · Iconic

Elderly women sitting outside their homes every morning hand-rolling orecchiette pasta. Watch, photograph, buy a bag of fresh pasta (~€3). Mornings are best (9am–12pm). Completely genuine, completely unforgettable.

📍 Map

⛪ Cattedrale di San Sabino

Free

Bari's magnificent Romanesque cathedral. Beautiful rose window, ancient crypt and remarkable icon of the Madonna Odegitria — one of the oldest in the world.

📍 Map
📍 Piazza dell'Odegitria · ⏰ 8am–12:30pm, 4–7pm · 💶 Free

🏰 Castello Svevo (Swabian Castle)

€4

A massive Norman-Swabian castle built by Frederick II in 1233. Two towers, a moat, impressive battlements and museum. Great views from the walls over Bari Vecchia and the sea.

📍 Map
📍 Piazza Federico II di Svevia · ⏰ 8:30am–7:30pm · 💶 €4pp

Places to visit in Matera

🪨 Sasso Caveoso

Must-See · Free

The older of Matera's two sassi districts — thousands of cave dwellings carved into the ravine, churches cut into the rock. Walk down into the ravine for the most dramatic views. One of the most extraordinary sights on Earth.

📍 Map

⛪ Cave Churches — Madonna de Idris

€3–5

Rock-carved churches with medieval frescoes still visible on the stone. Madonna de Idris is built into a rocky outcrop with extraordinary views. Completely unique and genuinely ancient.

📍 Map

🪨 Sasso Barisano

Free to walk

The more restored of the two sassi — hotels and restaurants now occupy many cave dwellings. Walk Via Madonna delle Virtù for the best views across the ravine.

📍 Map

📸 Belvedere di Murgia Timone

Best photo spot

The best viewpoint of Matera — from the opposite cliff across the ravine. Walk or taxi across the Gravina gorge. The view of thousands of cave dwellings cascading down is the most iconic image of Matera. Unmissable for photos.

📍 Map

💡 Top tips

Book Augsburg→Naples train ASAP

Munich→Rome leg especially — EC/Railjet trains book up fast. Nightjet sleeper cabins sell out weeks in advance.

Circumvesuviana — protect your bag

The Sorrento train is notorious for pickpockets. Keep valuables in a front zipped pocket at all times.

Blue Grotto — first thing on Capri day

Take the 8:35am ferry and go to the Blue Grotto immediately. Queues build by 10am and it closes when seas are rough.

Orecchiette is the must-eat in Puglia

Order orecchiette alle cime di rapa (orecchiette with turnip greens) everywhere in Bari, Alberobello and Matera. The definitive vegetarian dish of Puglia.

Matera bus — buy ticket day before

The FAL bus from Bari to Matera fills up on weekends. Go to the FAL ticket office at Bari station the day before to buy your return ticket.

Castel Sant'Elmo — go at sunset

Only €2.50 after 4pm and the golden-hour panorama over Naples Bay is the trip highlight. Don't miss it on Day 1.
